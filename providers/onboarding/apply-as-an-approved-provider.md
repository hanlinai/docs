# Apply as an approved provider

This page explains the provider application. It is intended for operators with data center capacity who want to serve frontier models or zero data retention traffic.

## About the application

Onboarding is permissionless. You do not need approval to claim a gateway key and onboard workers.

Approved providers are operators that Engy has reviewed. Engy can certify the workers of approved providers for zero data retention (ZDR) and for specific regions, so that they can serve users who require them.

## Apply

1. Go to [provider.engy.ai/become-a-provider](https://provider.engy.ai/become-a-provider).
2. Complete the form. It asks for the following information:
   * Team or operator name, contact email, and Telegram or Discord handle
   * Website
   * Data center and region
   * Hardware, including GPU model and count
   * Intended concurrency
   * Models that you intend to serve
   * Endpoint and OpenAI compatibility notes
   * Operating experience
   * Reason for applying
3. Click **Submit application**. If you are not signed in, click **Sign in to submit**, and sign in with your hotkey first. The application is linked to that hotkey.

Engy reviews each application. The status of an application is `pending`, `approved`, or `rejected`.

* **Pending**: You can edit the application and click **Update application**.
* **Rejected**: The page shows the reviewer's note. You can update the application and submit it again.
* **Approved**: The page shows a link to the dashboard. Submitting the form again does not change an approved status.

## After approval

Engy certifies workers for ZDR and regions individually. Certifications do not carry over when you switch a server to another model, because the switch creates a new worker.
